Slide 01 · Vision & Overview

NirogiQ: Care, with clarity.

A privacy-first healthcare companion designed to remove confusion from medicine routines using AI vision, clear scheduling, and multilingual voice reading.

🔍 The Packaging Problem

Blister packs feature tiny, shiny, curved text that elderly patients and caregivers constantly struggle to read.

💊 Medication Errors

Unclear prescriptions and missed reminder intervals lead to accidental double-dosing or missed vital care courses.

🌐 Language Gaps

Labels printed strictly in small English fonts exclude millions of regional and low-literacy users from independent care.

Speaker Note "Today, taking daily medications is needlessly stressful. NirogiQ transforms confusing blister packs into instant, readable text, simple alarms, and native voice audio."
Slide 02 · Feature 01

AI Label Reading & QR Scanner

Turning mobile camera snapshots of foil strips and bottles into verified medical information.

⚡ Gemini AI OCR Engine

Deep parsing extracts commercial name, strength (e.g. 500mg), active formula salt, and manufacturer within seconds.

🛡️ Confidence Badge & Safety

Tags text as 'High' or 'Not Sure'. Clearly instructs patients that AI assists transcription, not chemical verification.

📷 Instant QR Barcode Reader

Decodes package QR codes via in-browser jsQR scanning without downloading bulky third-party scanner apps.

✅ 1-Tap Verification & Save

Patients review the extracted fields and seamlessly push them directly into their daily routine list.

Speaker Note "Users simply take a photo. Google Gemini reads the label text—even on curved or torn packaging—and extracts the exact dosage strength without making wild guesses."
Slide 03 · Feature 02

Routine Tracker & Alarm Function

Structured timeline management that keeps today's doses front and center.

⏰ Time-of-Day Reminders

Set custom reminder hours for morning, afternoon, or evening routines matching the doctor's prescription.

📅 Prescription Date Range

Save start and finish dates so users know exactly when an antibiotic or short-term course should conclude.

🎯 "NEXT" Dose Prioritization

The dashboard intelligently computes and highlights the immediate next medication due, eliminating clutter.

Speaker Note "The routine tracker acts as the patient's command center. It highlights what is due next, sorts doses chronologically, and tracks prescription timelines."
Slide 04 · Feature 03

Multilingual Voice Accessibility

Making essential medical instructions audible for elderly and multilingual users.

🔊 1-Tap Speech Engine

A single tap reads aloud the drug name, reminder time, and special instructions at a calm, calibrated 0.88x speed.

🇮🇳 Native Regional Languages

Fully supports English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ) natively in the browser.

👵 Inclusive Design

Eliminates reading fatigue for seniors, visually impaired individuals, or family members administering care.

Speaker Note "Accessibility is at the heart of NirogiQ. Pressing the speaker button speaks the instructions clearly in Hindi, Punjabi, or English, ensuring true independence."
